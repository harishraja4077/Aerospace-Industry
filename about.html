Our Mission

Empowering Humanity's Journey to the Stars

At Stackly, our mission is to democratize access to space and advance aerospace technology for the betterment of humanity. We believe that the future of our species lies beyond Earth, and we're building the technology to get us there.

Innovation First

Every project begins with a question: "How can we do this better?" We challenge conventions and pioneer new approaches.

People-Centric

Our greatest asset is our team of world-class engineers, scientists, and visionaries working together.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Innovation

1991

Foundation

Stackly founded by Dr. Elena Vasquez with a vision to revolutionize commercial aerospace engineering.

2003

First Launch Success

Successfully launched our first orbital vehicle, AV-1 Pioneer, establishing credibility in the launch market.

2012

Global Expansion

Opened facilities in 12 countries, serving clients across 42 nations with a team of 1,500+ engineers.

2020

Quantum Comms Breakthrough

Achieved first quantum-encrypted satellite communication, setting a new standard for secure data transmission.

2026

Mars Mission Partnership

Selected as primary technology partner for the international Mars exploration program launching in 2028.
